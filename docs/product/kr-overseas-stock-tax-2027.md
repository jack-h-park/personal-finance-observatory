# 2027 Korean tax estimate for overseas stock sales

Open **Tax Planning → 2027 Korean tax estimate for overseas stock sales**. Enter annual
net gains or losses in KRW under two otherwise comparable scenarios, one without the
planned sale and one with it. The difference shows the change in Korean tax attributable
to that sale within the supported scope.

The estimate assumes Korean taxation already applies. A temporary overseas assignment
with continuing Korean tax residency can be modeled under that assumption. The
calculator makes no determination about personal residency or how the continuous
domestic domicile or abode test applies. The National Tax Service describes that test
in its [stock capital gains guidance](https://www.nts.go.kr/nts/cm/cntnts/cntntsView.do?cntntsId=8800&mi=12274).

## Supported scope and rules

- One taxpayer and one 2027 calendar year. Include only taxable overseas-listed common
  shares subject to the general rate. Each input is the complete annual net gain or
  loss under the scenario, already calculated in won, net of eligible costs and
  losses and before the basic deduction. Use explicit zero for no gain or loss.
- The same KRW 2,500,000 annual basic deduction can be shared with other taxable
  stock gains. This estimate assumes no other taxable stock disposals and applies
  the deduction once to each independent scenario. A loss or a gain at or below the
  deduction produces a zero tax base. See the [Income Tax Act, Articles 94, 102,
  103 and 104](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?efYd=20270101&lsiSeq=280405)
  and the National Tax Service guidance above.
- Apply 20% national capital gains tax under Income Tax Act Article 104(1)(12)(b)
  and the 2% standard local rate under [Local Tax Act Article 103-3(1)(12)(b)](https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsLawLinkInfo.do?chrClsCd=010202&lsId=001649&lsJoLnkSeq=1000899162&print=print)
  separately to the same positive tax base. The local base follows Local Tax Act
  Article 103-2(1). The estimate rounds each component to the nearest won, half up,
  sums the rounded components, and subtracts the baseline from the scenario. The
  whole-won estimate uses a planning convention and excludes filing quantization.
  Check the law in force at the time of sale.
- Mixed-rate stock disposals, eligible SME rates, funds and derivatives, special
  relief, foreign tax credits, treaty treatment, US liability, lot accounting,
  transaction-level currency conversion, prior-year losses and settlement are out
  of scope. Browser-tab state is the only place entered values are held. The route
  uses no financial records and performs no writes or filed-return comparisons.

## Synthetic coverage

The tests cover the deduction boundary, net losses, a tax decrease, an unchanged
zero, distinct national and local components, rounding, malformed input and a gain
large enough to distinguish the general overseas rate from domestic progressive rates.
Moving from KRW 2,000,000 to KRW 12,000,000 of annual eligible net gains produces an
estimated increase of KRW 2,090,000 (KRW 1,900,000 national and KRW 190,000 local).
